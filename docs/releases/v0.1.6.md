## Player controls

- The title bar shows the installed version below the Andiya name.
- Selecting an active sidebar button closes its panel. Select **Playing** to
  close all open sidebars.
- Playback controls show **Play** or **Pause**. Space toggles playback after you
  click out of a search field, and holding the key triggers a single toggle.
- Menus fit their labels and wrap longer entries when space is limited.

## Downloads

- **Windows x64** offers a per-user installer and a portable ZIP.
- **macOS Apple Silicon** offers an ad-hoc signed DMG.
- **Linux x86_64** offers a TAR.GZ archive and a DEB package.

SHA-256 checksums accompany the downloads.
